# Christmas Pockle concepts

Four separate imagegen character sheets for the owner's Meshy workflow. Each has front, side, back and three-quarter references. Working names only; these characters have not been added to the catalog or generated as 3D assets. The upper-left front view is the clearest identity reference; the other views explain shape and attached details.

Every imported model must support animated eyes/mouth and a character-specific idle with a calm alternative. Proposed motion: Merry Fir sways with a soft star bob; Cocoa Cub gently bounces and tilts; Ginger Puff hops and waves; Snow Sprout sways and gives a small mitten wave. Keep accessories integrated and provide neutral expressions so future audio events can drive emotion. These sheets do not constitute completed rigs or animation.

A few chunky cranberry-red and warm-gold molded baubles, identical placement in every view. No arms or branch spikes.
