Get Pockle

Little toys. Big squish.

Pockle is a collecting game of original designer toys you can squish, stretch and play with. Walk to earn boxes, open them to find out who's inside, and build your shelf.

Walk, open, play

  1. Walk

    Every 1,000 steps earns a free box. A new one is ready every six hours, up to four a day. Walking is the free way to collect.

  2. Open

    The box shows which collection it's from, but not which toy. Tap it, it shakes, and someone new pops out.

  3. Play

    Every Pockle reacts to your touch. Jelly wobbles, plush squashes, vinyl spins and shines, and each one has its own personality.

Every finish feels different

Pockles come in translucent jelly, soft plush, glossy and matte vinyl, glitter gel and pearl. The finish changes more than the colour: it changes how a toy bounces, squishes and catches the light.

  • Jelly
  • Plush
  • Vinyl
  • Glitter gel

One character, one series

Each series is one character in many finishes. Wally's has fifteen: the same round whale in fifteen different looks.

  • The box tells you the series, not the toy. You always know whose box you're opening. The finish is the surprise.
  • Earlier series stay around. A series is available until it runs out, and the newest one is always shown first.
  • Vintage counts. Every toy remembers which series it came from and the day you found it.

Common, Rare, Epic and Secret

Most finishes in a series are Common. Fewer are Rare, fewer still are Epic, and a series can hide a single Secret. Wally is the first series with all four.

  • Common

    The everyday heroes of a series.

  • Rare

    Gilded and golden.

  • Epic

    Crystal-cut and glowing.

  • Secret

    The one you'll show everyone.

Before you buy a box, the app shows the exact chances for that box. They're based on how many of each tier are left, so they change as copies run out.

When they're gone, they're gone

Every finish is made in a fixed number of copies, worldwide. Rarer tiers have fewer.

Copies made of each finish, by tier

Each copy carries its own number, so yours might be #412 of 6,000. When a series sells out, no more are made. You keep yours, play with it, and can still trade it.

No subscriptions, no coins and no ads in the way.

Two spares, one new toy

Have two spare copies of the same character? Fuse them. The result is a new Pockle that blends both finishes, with a name and personality of its own.

Both spares are used up, and you always keep at least one of each original.

Swap your spares

Trade spare toys with other collectors, one toy for another. You both see exactly what's being swapped, and you both confirm before anything moves.

Your last copy of a toy is never up for trade, and no money is involved, only toys. Sold-out finishes can still change hands, so a series never really ends.

Trading uses preset messages only, and you can block or report anyone.

Boxes along the way

Pockle counts your steps and nothing else. Every 1,000 steps unlocks a box, so an ordinary walk turns into a few surprises.

Collecting, kept fair

  • Odds are shown before every purchase.
  • Packs buy more boxes, not better odds.
  • Toys can't be sold for money, in the game or outside it.
  • A paid box is saved to your account before it opens, so a dropped connection never loses it or charges you twice.
  • Pockle is for ages 13 and up.

Questions

Is Pockle free?

Yes. You can collect by walking without paying anything. Boxes can also be bought.

Do I have to walk?

No, but walking is how you earn free boxes. Pockle only uses your step count, not your location.

Can I get duplicates?

Yes. Duplicates become spares you can fuse or trade.

What happens when a series sells out?

No new copies are made. Everyone who has one keeps it, and it can still be traded.

Can I see the odds?

Always, before you buy. They update as copies run out.

What do I sign in with?

Apple or Google. Your collection is saved to your account.

Which phones does it run on?

iPhone and Android.

How do I delete my account?

From the app's settings, or on our account deletion page.
