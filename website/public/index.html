How it works

Walk, open, collect, swap.

Walk for free boxes

Every 1,000 steps fills a box, and a new one is ready every few hours. No purchase needed.

Fill your shelf

Every toy has its own finish and personality. Squish jelly, spin vinyl, chase the rare golds, diamonds and secrets.

Trade with friends

Swap spares toy-for-toy to finish a collection. Trades are made with taps, never money.

Collections

Eleven characters, dozens of finishes.

For parents

Made for players 13 and up.

Pockle asks for a birthday before anyone can play and doesn't keep it. Players talk with preset reactions instead of open chat, and trades are toy-for-toy only.

  • No open chat or free-text comments
  • Step counts only, never your location
  • Box odds shown before every box
