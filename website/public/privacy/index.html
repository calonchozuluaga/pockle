The short version

  • Pockle is a collecting game for people 13 and older, made by Zulapp LLC.
  • We collect what the game needs: your sign-in, your collector name, your toys, your step counts and your purchases.
  • We don't collect your location, your contacts or your photos, and we don't keep your birthday.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app or on this website.

We don't collect precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. Players can't send each other free-text messages.

How we use it

  • To provide the game: accounts, cloud saves, boxes, trading and notifications.
  • To keep Pockle fair and safe: verifying steps and purchases, preventing cheating, enforcing our Terms and handling reports.
  • To understand and improve the game using aggregated, de-identified statistics.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We don't use your information for targeted advertising, and we don't sell it.

What other players can see

Your collector name, avatar, the shelf you choose to show, your badges, and the reactions you send are visible to friends, and to other players when you trade. The public trading ledger on this website shows how many of each toy exist and are still available, and open trade offers and wants. It never shows email addresses, sign-in details or step counts.

Shelves are private until you choose to make yours public.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us run Pockle, under contracts that limit how they use it:

  • Supabase — database, accounts and game servers.
  • Apple and Google — sign-in, app distribution, in-app purchases and push notifications.
  • Vercel — hosting for this website.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect players' safety, or as part of a sale or merger of Zulapp LLC (you would be told first).

Children and teens

Pockle is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. The birthday check blocks anyone under 13 from creating an account. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact privacy@playpockle.com and we will delete it.

Players aged 13 to 17 should have a parent or guardian's permission to play and to make purchases.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify your personal information within 30 days, except purchase and transaction records we must keep for tax, accounting or fraud-prevention reasons, and anonymous toy-count statistics on the public ledger.

Your choices and rights

  • Access, correct or delete your information: use the in-app settings, the delete account page, or email privacy@playpockle.com.
  • Step counting and notifications can be turned off at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Union), you may have additional rights, such as to know what we hold, to object, or to port your data. Email privacy@playpockle.com and we'll respond within the time the law requires.

Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and server-side checks to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your Apple or Google account secure too.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll update this page and tell you in the app before they take effect.
