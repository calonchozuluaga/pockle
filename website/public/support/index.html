Common questions

How do I get free boxes?

Turn on walking in the app. Every 1,000 steps fills a box, and a new box can be earned every 6 hours, so up to four a day. If you miss one, up to two wait for you.

What are the box odds?

Every box shows its odds before you open or buy it, including the chance of rare gold, diamond and secret toys. Paid boxes only give toys you don't own yet.

I got a new phone. How do I get my toys back?

Install Pockle and sign in with the same Apple or Google account you used before. Your shelf, spares, fusions and badges come with you.

How does trading work?

Offer a spare to a friend or post it on the public ledger. In the trade room you can accept, decline, counter, or ask for a sweeter deal. Trades are toy-for-toy, expire after 24 hours, and both players confirm before anything moves.

My steps aren't counting.

Check that Pockle has permission to read physical activity (Android) or Motion & Fitness (iPhone) in your phone's settings, and that walking is turned on in Pockle's settings.

I bought a box but didn't get it.

Close and reopen Pockle while connected to the internet; it will finish delivering. If it still hasn't arrived, email support@playpockle.com with the time of purchase and your collector name.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap their collector name, then Report or Block. Blocked players can't send you trades or reactions.

Can I change my collector name?

Yes, from your profile. Names must be 3–16 letters, numbers or underscores, and must not be your real name or offensive.

For parents

Pockle is for players 13 and older. There's no open chat: players react with preset stickers and phrases, and trades are toy-for-toy with no money involved. Purchases go through Apple or Google, so you can use Screen Time or Google Family Link to require approval. To ask about or delete a teen's account, email privacy@playpockle.com.

Contact us

Email support@playpockle.com with your collector name and what happened. We usually reply within two business days.
