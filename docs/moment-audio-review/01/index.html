How these should behave

One cue per moment. A special reveal replaces the standard reveal. A ready walking box takes priority over the 1,000-step cue when both happen together. The saved-delivery confirmation is optional and should stay silent when a reveal starts immediately.

Fusion, purchase and trade cues play only after the result is saved. Respect the app sound preference, preserve the whole tail, and keep all playback local. Trade and purchase-confirmation audio are imported but remain unbound; the current purchase flow uses its reveal cue.
