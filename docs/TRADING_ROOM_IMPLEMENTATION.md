# Trading room — public offers marketplace

November 8, 2026 · UX-036 / UX-029 / UX-035 · design and source audit only. **Owner selected a public offers marketplace**, replacing the earlier invite-code proposal. Trading is required for launch. No trading service, marketplace, inventory migration or remote database change was implemented in this pass.

Collectors browse public “I have / I want” offers, filter by collection and rarity, and exchange one eligible owned spare for a specific wanted original finish. Listing terms are fixed; the server exchanges the two exact copies in one transaction. No money, free-text chat, gifts, multi-toy sweeteners or contacts upload in this first implementation. The same ownership foundation must govern fusion: adding a marketplace over local counts would allow the same spare to be spent twice. Fused-toy and legacy/beta trade eligibility remain owner decisions; this plan does not approve either.

## Marketplace behavior

1. **Browse offers.** Signed-in collectors see open, unexpired offers with “Gives / Wants,” collection and rarity on both sides, and the creator's public collector name. Support collection/rarity filters for either side and “I can trade” computed from the caller's eligible spares. Use paginated server filtering; never download another collector's whole inventory. Do not show cash values, private identity, location or a social inbox.
2. **Publish fixed terms.** Select one eligible server-owned spare and one wanted original catalog finish. Show the final “You give / You want” preview. Explicit **Publish offer** confirms those exact terms, including that another collector can complete this trade while the maker is offline. Publishing reserves the offered copy and preserves at least one unreserved copy of that finish. A reserved spare cannot be fused, traded or listed elsewhere. No listing becomes open before the reservation and terms commit together.
3. **Do not edit a live offer.** Store immutable offered copy, wanted finish, environment, policy revision and `offer_version`/terms digest. To change terms, cancel and publish a new offer with a new ID. Old previews or confirmations must never authorize the replacement. Status/revision changes do not silently change the toys.
4. **Review and accept.** An eligible counterparty chooses one exact spare matching the wanted finish, reviews “You give / You receive,” and confirms with the observed offer ID/version/digest. Recommended first flow: the server atomically revalidates, reserves/consumes that selection and swaps both copies in this one acceptance transaction. The maker's publication plus the taker's final acceptance are the two explicit consents. Do not require the offline maker to approve a second time unless the product deliberately changes to a separate claim-and-confirm flow.
5. **Optional claim step, if the UI needs it.** A short, server-owned claim may reserve the taker's copy and block other accepts while the taker confirms. Claim and final confirmation must use the same immutable offer version, exact copy and account. The server must release both claim state and taker reservation when the claim expires/cancels, then reopen only a still-valid maker listing. Claim TTL must be distinct from listing TTL. This is an implementation alternative, not an additional promise or a required first-release feature.
6. **Complete or recover.** Return a durable trade receipt with both updated inventory revisions. Show the received toy with **Play** and **Back to shelf**. A lost response/restart fetches that same receipt; never apply an additive grant again. Public offer state becomes completed/unavailable immediately after commit.
7. **Cancel/expire.** The maker may cancel an uncompleted offer. Propose a visible 24-hour listing expiry, consistent with the earlier design. Cancellation/expiry releases its reservation exactly once. If cancellation races acceptance, one transaction wins; a completed swap cannot be cancelled. Blocking prevents new matches and cancels any uncommitted claim between those players; account deletion closes that account's offers/claims. No forced undo of a completed trade is promised.

Public means discoverable to other eligible collectors in the app; it does not require an anonymous public website endpoint. Expose only the collector name, opaque listing ID, offered/wanted catalog metadata, terms version and expiry needed to browse. Keep exact internal copy IDs, player/Auth IDs, emails, receipts and inventory totals private. Completion receipts are participant-only. Filter either-direction blocks on browse/detail as well as acceptance. Offer/detail screens need **Block** and preset-reason **Report**; reporting is private to moderation. Apply creation, browse, accept and report rate limits and a bounded number of live offers per player. Final limits/TTL are tuning proposals, not approved values.

## Ownership foundation and transaction contract

Use private server tables with RLS as defense in depth; clients receive filtered DTOs through the existing authenticated Edge pattern. Never accept an actor/owner ID from a client as authority. Restrict function execution and use invoker functions/fixed search paths consistently with the existing backend. Supabase's [function permission guidance](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/functions) and [RLS guidance](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/postgres/row-level-security) were reviewed for this plan.

- `copies`: immutable ID, collectible ID, source/environment, optional production serial, current owner, active/reserved/consumed state. Production finite-edition allocation is separate from sandbox fixtures and legacy preservation.
- `inventory_operations`: actor, kind, idempotency key, canonical request hash and immutable result; the same key with changed terms fails.
- `copy_reservations`: unique active reservation per exact copy, purpose and expiry. Fusion, trade and any other spend must share these locks.
- `trade_listings`: maker, exact offered copy, wanted original collectible ID, environment/policy, immutable offer version/hash, status, creation/expiry and committed receipt reference. Index open listing collection/rarity filters and maker; only a safe DTO is publicly browsable.
- `trade_acceptances`: listing, taker, exact matching spare, observed offer version/hash, confirmation time and operation/result. If the optional claim flow is chosen, add short claim expiry/status and unique active claim per listing; do not treat a claim as a completed swap.
- `inventory_events`: append-only mint, reserve/release, fusion-consume, trade-out/in and approved correction events. Current inventory revisions are server-generated.
- `blocks` / `reports`: block checked at browse/detail, publish/accept and final commit; reporting does not reveal private account identifiers.

The commit locks both player rows in deterministic order, then the listing and participating copies/reservations in the globally agreed order. Recheck the current taker session, both accounts' eligibility, expiry, blocks, maker's published consent, taker's matching confirmation, immutable terms, owners, reservation purpose and last-copy invariants under those locks. The maker need not have a currently online session: its stored offer authorization is explicit, bounded and cancellable. Swap both owners, append both events, advance both inventory revisions, consume reservations and save the result in **one database transaction**. No separate “remove A, add B” network calls. Expiry, cancellation, fusion, refund handling and deletion must use the same ordering and rechecks.

Suggested service actions: `list_offers`, `offer_detail`, `create_offer`, `accept_offer`, `cancel_offer`, `my_offers`, `my_trade_history`, plus optional `claim_offer` / `confirm_claim` / `release_claim` only if that flow is chosen. Mutation requests carry an idempotency key and expected offer version/digest; responses carry server state/version, inventory revisions and a receipt when complete. Provide stable codes for `not_spare`, `copy_reserved`, `wanted_finish_mismatch`, `terms_changed`, `offer_expired`, `offer_unavailable`, `blocked`, `account_disabled`, `trade_completed` and `unavailable`. Refresh visible offers conservatively, or add authorized Realtime later; events only trigger a fresh server read and never grant ownership.

The Unity cache must replace/reconcile a committed server snapshot by revision. It must not union old count snapshots into the authoritative count, resurrect spent copies, or replay delivery receipts into copies that already traded/fused away. Keep legacy play inventory separately labelled. A server-owned copy cannot be fused offline while it remains eligible for trading elsewhere.

## Acceptance — all still unrun for trading

- Browse/filter accurately by offered/wanted collection and rarity; unavailable, expired and either-direction blocked offers never appear as actionable. Public DTOs reveal only intended collector/listing/catalog fields, not inventory, account identifiers or receipts.
- Two real accounts exchange one eligible spare each; both retain their last copy. Exact copy IDs/serials keep their history. Reserved, consumed, wrong-environment and policy-unapproved copy classes fail without mutation; fused/legacy coverage follows the explicit final eligibility decision.
- Publishing fixes the terms and reserves the offered spare atomically. Multiple takers, duplicate requests and changed idempotency keys cannot swap twice. A cancelled/replaced offer cannot inherit confirmation. Self-trade and private copy/receipt reads by a third party are denied.
- If claims are added, they bind the same offer version, matching exact copy and account; expiry releases the taker copy without losing the maker's valid offer. Changing terms is not possible within a claim.
- Race final acceptance against fusion, another offer, cancellation, expiry, block, account deletion and refund state. At most one operation spends a given spare; failure leaves neither swap leg committed.
- Kill the app or network before/after the final response. Resume the same committed receipt and revisions; the received toy survives restart and plays immediately. Refreshing an older device cannot restore a traded/fused copy.
- RLS/privilege checks deny direct client writes and unauthorized private reads; public browse exposes only the approved DTO. Rate limits, offer/claim expiry and pagination work. Reports contain only intended evidence and are accessible only to moderation.
- Sound preference and notification preference gates apply; completion sound/badge appears once after persistence. No repeated trade-success presentation on ordinary refresh.
