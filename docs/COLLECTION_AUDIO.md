November3,2026. Owner revisits the previously agreed themed sound sets per collection (Claude handoff Oct21). This is the production plan, not generated audio or an ElevenLabs account/credit authorization.

## Verified starting point

TactilePrototype creates four shared procedural clips (press, release, landing and reveal). No WAV/MP3/OGG/AIFF collection assets were found under Assets/Pockle. ## Recommended layers

1. **Character identity:** six short nonverbal cues per collection: greeting/arrival, happy, curious, surprised, playful action, sleepy. Distinct timbre per character, with personality choosing appropriate expressive delivery. No spoken dialogue or cloned third-party character voice. Several quiet variations for common cues avoid robotic repetition; audible QA comes before expanding variants.
2. **Material feel:** reusable tactile assets for gel squish/release/swirl, vinyl tap/wobble/spin, plush brush/rustle and gentle placement. These follow the finish/material, while the character identity stays recognizable. Fused materials select/blend a reviewed tactile profile rather than changing the character's entire voice.
3. **Box and tier reveal:** carton open/unfold, toy arrival and a shared Common/Rare/Epic/Secret musical identity with collection-specific accents. Calm/skip/restart behavior must avoid overlapping or replaying celebratory audio in a loop. A sound is not a proof of payment/delivery.

Examples to audition: Pip soft bubbly peeps, Fox playful chirrups, Wally round airy whale-like coos; axolotl tiny watery squeaks. These are proposed directions, not approved recordings. Sound should be gentle at phone-speaker volume, with no startling Secret blasts or repetitive begging/pressure to buy.

## Production path

ElevenLabs Sound Effects is the recommended first tool to audition for custom nonverbal character sounds and signature effects. It supports text-described effects and duration controls. Consistent character timbre is an audition/editing requirement, not guaranteed by the generator. Tactile Foley can use generated, original recorded or appropriately licensed source clips when they sound more convincing.

Start with Pip/Fox/Wally pilot sets, review actual audio on the phone with expressions, then expand the same event list to all11 collections (66core identity clips before alternates, plus material/reveal banks). No generation spending or paid subscription was approved in this discussion. Verify commercial asset rights/source records before shipping. Trim/normalize/edit and test low-volume phone playback; do not infer success from waveforms or filenames.

All shipped clips play locally with no per-touch AI/network call. Honor mute/volume, sensible voice limits, minimum cue spacing and non-repeating selection. Tie sound events to actual animation moments and facial expression state; prefer reviewed expression poses/cue timing before considering phoneme-style lip sync. Preserve intentional quiet idle periods.
