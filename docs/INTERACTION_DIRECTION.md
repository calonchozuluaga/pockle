# Pockle viewer direction

The current source includes direct touch for squeezing/lifting, two-finger directional squish/stretch, accelerometer jiggle, and an exposed plate rim for rotation. Only touches starting on the visible toy can join a toy pinch; either surviving finger continues a rebased one-finger drag. Turning stops on release. There are no Touch/Rotate mode buttons. The redesigned Jelly Garden carton now opens its hinged lid/flaps and reveals the selected Pip before enabling touch. The glossy authored shell uses a static studio environment; the newest Unity appearance and Android behavior still need owner testing. Follow [the manipulation checklist](MANIPULATION_TEST.md) and [box-opening checklist](BOX_REVEAL.md).

The owner's next viewer ideas are recorded here without claiming they are implemented:

- **Further sensor/filling refinement:** the first accelerometer pass drives bounded jelly wobble; contents follow that deformation. Independent filling inertia and optional gyroscope orientation reactions can extend it after the phone test. Sensor reactions are toy interaction, while walking rewards retain separate validation.
- **Reactive filling:** gold stars and pearls mostly follow a squeeze/stretch, lag behind the gel, then drift briefly and settle after release. Keep pieces inside the changing shell. Begin with a few readable inclusions instead of a full fluid simulation.
- **Lights-off glow:** selectable lighting environments, beginning with a simple lights-off control for glowing editions. Combine a luminous material with a subtle halo and grounded colored glow; emission alone does not illuminate the surrounding scene. Material and reflection settings must respond to the chosen environment.
- **Wet outer shell:** target the approved jelly concept's broad white reflections, clear warm rim, layered filling, rounded lower edge, and soft contact shadow. The current static reflection pass is an approximation. Test on Android before introducing expensive scene refraction.

The collecting direction is daily collection-themed boxes unlocked by walking, plus optional paid boxes at proposed standard/special price points. See [the product brief](PRODUCT_BRIEF.md). The current viewer and [packaging concepts](packaging/README.md) do not implement walking validation, payments, ownership, or inventory.

Packaging refinement: the owner liked the first collection concepts but asked for a custom soft, squishy Pockle wordmark and packaging that reads as a collectible surprise toy box. The revised visual direction uses playful mystery silhouettes, question marks, and collection motifs, while keeping collection identity explicit. This is a branding exploration before producing final logo vectors or applying the design to Unity.
