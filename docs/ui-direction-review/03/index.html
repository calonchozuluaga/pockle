FIVE DESTINATIONS APPROVED · NEW BAR FOR REVIEW

Grounded. Clear. Always in reach.

The menu meets both edges and the bottom of the screen. Five equal targets, new icons and quiet selected states. Your toys scroll above the bar, with every last row reachable.

01 / YOUR SHELF

Every toy. One place.

No collection folders between you and play.

Try it: select Fusions or Extras, search a character, then tap a toy.

One tile per owned finish, with extra copies counted on that tile. Fusions belong in this same grid. Walk and Shop help you grow your shelf; Fuse lives here, not in the global menu.

02 / COMMUNITY

A place to belong.

Feed, friends and public exchanges together.

Try it: switch Feed / Friends / Exchange and inspect an offer.

Examples are clearly labeled. Publishing an offer confirms the owner's fixed terms; acceptance must check both exact spare copies before any real exchange. Social services remain unimplemented.

03 / YOUR IDENTITY

Make it feel like you.

Pick a little face. Or bring your own.

Try it: choose an avatar or upload an image and adjust its crop.

The avatar opens Profile from every destination. Settings stay there. This prototype keeps an uploaded image only in this browser page's memory; it never sends or saves the file.

TWELVE LITTLE FACES

One family. Plenty of personality.

New avatar artwork for review, shown large and as a true 48px circle. Choose any face to try it throughout the phone prototypes. Original images are unchanged; the circular crop is CSS only.

THE APPROVED FIVE · A FULL-WIDTH BAR

Rooted at the bottom.
Room for every destination.

No floating container, outer pill or raised center button. A continuous plum surface reaches both edges and continues through the home-indicator area. Profile and Settings stay behind your avatar; fusion stays in Shelf.

ONE CONSISTENT COMPONENT

Big enough to tap.
Quiet enough to belong.

  • Five equal-width targets with visible labels.
  • Regular icons at rest; filled icons when selected.
  • A small peach highlight behind the selected icon.
  • Content ends above the bar; the last row stays clear.
  • The native home indicator sits on the same plum surface.

Try any tab in the phones above. These are proposed HTML screens, not an installed app update.

ONE ICON FAMILY · TWO CLEAR STATES

Familiar shapes.
A little more character.

A house, a shelf grid, a moving sneaker, a group of people and a storefront. Every icon comes from the same Phosphor set; the labels carry the destination names.

KEEP THE GOOD PARTS

Live play, material detail, expressions and sound stay one tap away. This pass reorganizes the paths around them.
