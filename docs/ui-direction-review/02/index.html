RECOMMENDED

A warm world, with room to breathe.

Peach surfaces, plum actions and the real toys carry the personality. The native status bar stays visible, and the bottom menu has enough inset space for all five labels.

01 / YOUR SHELF

Every toy. One place.

No collection folders between you and play.

Try it: select Fusions or Extras, search a character, then tap a toy.

One tile per owned finish, with extra copies counted on that tile. Fusions belong in this same grid. Walk and Shop help you grow your shelf; Fuse lives here, not in the global menu.

02 / COMMUNITY

A place to belong.

Feed, friends and public exchanges together.

Try it: switch Feed / Friends / Exchange and inspect an offer.

Examples are clearly labeled. Publishing an offer confirms the owner's fixed terms; acceptance must check both exact spare copies before any real exchange. Social services remain unimplemented.

03 / YOUR IDENTITY

Make it feel like you.

Pick a little face. Or bring your own.

Try it: choose an avatar or upload an image and adjust its crop.

The avatar opens Profile from every destination. Settings stay there. This prototype keeps an uploaded image only in this browser page's memory; it never sends or saves the file.

TWELVE LITTLE FACES

One family. Plenty of personality.

New avatar artwork for review, shown large and as a true 48px circle. Choose any face to try it throughout the phone prototypes. Original images are unchanged; the circular crop is CSS only.

TWO NAVIGATION DIRECTIONS

Same size. Same weight.
No floating Fuse button.

Both options keep Profile and Settings behind your avatar, and put fusion inside Shelf. Every tab has a readable label and an equal tap area.
