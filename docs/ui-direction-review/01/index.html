A little more world.
A lot more Pockle.

Bring the collection artwork into the app. Rich plum controls, peach light and colorful toys give every screen a place of its own.

The direction I recommend

Warm and colorful for everyday play. Deeper twilight surfaces for special moments, with the same familiar layout.

Switch the palette to compare all five screens. Tap “View contents” or “Get a Pip box” to see the proposed purchase sheet. Use the Trading room filters and “View offer” to explore the public-market concept. All names, counts and offers below are sample UI; no actions affect the game.

What changes across the app

Color with a job to do.

  • Peach and plum become the foundation.Keep pale surfaces for readable content, surrounded by world art and richer color.
  • Every collection brings its own atmosphere.Covers, environment crops and accent colors tie browsing to play.
  • Make the next action obvious.Play after fusion. Get a box inside a collection. Check contents before paying.
  • Reserve the biggest glow for the biggest moments.Fusions, reveals and milestones get richer lighting and short sound cues.
