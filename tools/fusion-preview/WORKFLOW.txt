Pip fusion review gallery, pass 01

Scope: all unordered combinations with replacement of the ten original Pip finishes: 55 total (45 cross-finish + 10 same-finish). Existing fusion studies are outcomes, not extra parents. The app's catalog, inventory and phone build are unchanged.

Regenerate:
  python3 tools/fusion-preview/build-pip-gallery.py
  Run Unity6000.6.5f1 on an isolated project with current owner Assets, using:
  -executeMethod Pockle.Editor.PipFusionGalleryCapture.Capture -fusionGallery <absolute path to docs/fusion-review/pip-01> -quit
  The capture expects recipes.json and writes 165 PNGs to renders/. Copy the ten original finish captures from docs/captures/latest-pip-collection into parents/ if creating a new output folder.

Open docs/fusion-review/pip-01/index.html. Use Parent or Rule filters; choose Front, Turned or Dark. The matrix mirrors unordered pairs for readability; mirrored cells open the same canonical result. Keep/Rework marks use browser localStorage; export a JSON review before changing browsers. If storage is blocked, the page displays a warning. Exported decisions are not applied to the app automatically.

Rule proposal:
- Preserve parent palettes as gradients or marbling, rather than average RGB.
- Same-finish pairs deepen pigment and strengthen signature material cues.
- Textile takes priority as a soft/fuzzy surface treatment.
- Foam produces matte marbling or soft-touch coated surfaces.
- Rigid parents produce a coated shell or a jelly glaze.
- Jelly combinations retain translucent/pearl/sparkle cues.
- Glow adds bounded local emission to the selected surface treatment.
- Dusk, Sorbet, Honeydew and Eclipse use their current phone material factories, preserving actual existing study appearance.

All personality labels and reaction descriptions are draft proposals, keeping Pip curious and sunny. No new animation is generated. Same-character restriction, selection-order independence and same-finish coverage are the review assumptions. Re-fusion, production recipe rules, personality inheritance and rarity remain separate decisions. Fuzz approximates pile, not individual fibers. Dark images show the shader on a dark field without an in-game halo or painted environment. The gallery intentionally exposes similar-looking outcomes so the owner can mark them for refinement.
