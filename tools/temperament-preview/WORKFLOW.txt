Pip temperament face studies — pass02 (editor-only)

1. Run python3 tools/temperament-preview/build-pip-faces.py.
2. Use Unity 6000.6.5f1 on an isolated copy of the current owner project:
   Unity -batchmode -projectPath <snapshot> -executeMethod Pockle.Editor.PipTemperamentFaceCapture.Capture -facePreview <absolute docs/personality-review/pip-02> -quit -logFile <log>
3. Convert frames/<temperament>/000.png through 063.png into looping GIFs
   at 0.07 seconds/frame, saved as loops/<temperament>.gif. The native
   macOS ImageIO encoder supplied here avoids external dependencies:
   swift -module-cache-path /private/tmp/pip-swift-cache tools/temperament-preview/encode-loops.swift <absolute output folder>
4. Inspect rest, tap and signature stills and transition frames. Keep frame
   sequences in the isolated capture directory; publish renders, loops,
   poses.json and index.html only.

Pass01 output is preserved for comparison; the current tool generates pass02.
Top-cut/angled lids, curved closure, stronger gaze, round mouths, crooked grin,
double/star glints and lower central blush implement Claude’s Oct15 review.

Eight temperaments; 24 poses; 48 stills; eight 64-frame loops. The shader
authors local face paint and smooths front shading as a feasibility proof.
Sculpted eye outlines may remain faintly visible when closed: production
face integration still needs cleanup. This does not modify source textures,
meshes, runtime shaders, saves, personality assignment or phone builds.
